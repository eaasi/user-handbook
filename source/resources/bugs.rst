Reporting Bugs
=================

.. warning::
     Please be aware that the v2021.10 EAASI codebase is no longer under active development and will only be supported for Yale University community members and selected partners until Next-Gen EAASI is ready for active deployment. Suggestions for the Next-Gen EAASI roadmap based on your experience with v2021.10 are welcome, but there will be no updates to the v2021.10 code - including bug fixes.

EAASI users at Yale or who have access to EAASI via selected partnerships may contact the Software Preservation and Emulation team (softwarepreservation@yale.edu) for technical support, including any questions about expected functionality in the legacy `v2021.10` codebase and work-arounds for known bugs. The team will triage and respond to your report as soon as possible.
