Reporting Bugs
=================

.. warning::
     Please be aware that the latest EAASI codebase is under active development and not yet ready for use or testing.

EAASI users at Yale or who have access to EAASI via selected partnerships may contact the Software Preservation and Emulation team (softwarepreservation@yale.edu) for technical support. The team will triage and respond to your report as soon as possible.
