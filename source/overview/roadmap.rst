EAASI Product Roadmap
======================

EAASI development is now tracked on the platform's GitHub. Please consult the `EAASI Current Roadmap and Idea Backlog <https://github.com/orgs/eaasi/projects/5/views/6>`_ for up-to-date progress on the work that has been prioritized by the Yale Software Preservation and Emulation unit.

The day-to-day effort of team members to address the roadmap is broken down in further detail on the `EAASI Prioitized Issue Tracker <https://github.com/orgs/eaasi/projects/3/views/1>`_ for even greater transparency. Please reach out to the Software Preservation and Emulation team (softwarepreservation@yale.edu) if you have any questions about the product roadmap or how EAASI dev works!

.. warning::
    All of the features and areas of work detailed in the Current Roadmap and Idea Backlog refer to the "latest", re-architected version of the EAASI platform - what we're referring to as "Next-Gen EAASI." Please be aware that the v2021.10 EAASI codebase is no longer under active development and is only supported for Yale University and selected partners until Next-Gen EAASI is ready for active deployment. Suggestions for the Next-Gen EAASI roadmap based on experience with v2021.10 or general requirements for emulation are welcome, but please do not expect any direct updates to v2021.10 functionality.
